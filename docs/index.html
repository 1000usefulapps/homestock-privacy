Privacy Policy — Home stock (“Homestock”)

Last updated: May 7, 2026

This policy describes how the Home stock mobile application (package com.myhome.homestock, “the App”) handles information. The App is offered by the publisher responsible for the Google Play listing (“we” / “us”).

Summary

The App is designed to work primarily on your device. Inventory data you enter is stored locally (on-device database). We do not operate a login or a central server for your stock lists. Some features use Google services (ads, optional in-app purchases, listings on Google Play) under Google’s policies.

Information the App processes

Data you provide (stored on device)

  • Item names, quantities, thresholds, categories, notes
  • Optional photos you attach (stored in app-private storage)
  • App settings (language, reminder time, notification preferences, etc.)
  • Shopping list entries (Premium), including quantities to buy — stored locally

This data remains on your phone or tablet unless you back up or transfer it using tools provided by your device manufacturer (e.g. Android backup) or third-party apps, which are outside our control.

Advertising (free version)

Where the free version shows ads, Google AdMob may use identifiers and device data to show and measure ads, subject to your consent where required (e.g. UMP / GDPR / ePrivacy flows in the App) and your device settings. For details, see Google’s Privacy & Terms.

You can limit ad personalization in Android settings (e.g. Google → Ads) and through in-app consent options where available.

In-app purchases (Premium)

Purchases handled by Google Play Billing are processed by Google. We do not receive your full payment card details. Google may provide us limited purchase verification signals required to unlock features. See Google Payments Privacy.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, the App schedules local notifications about low or empty stock. We do not need a server to send these. System notification permission is used only to show these alerts.

Diagnostics

In debug / internal builds, diagnostic features may collect more detail for development; release builds distributed through Google Play are configured for end users without extra debug instrumentation, except as needed for ads, billing, and platform services described above.

Third-party services

The App may use or link to:

Links to websites (e.g. this policy) may be opened in your browser. Those sites may set their own cookies or logs.

Data retention and deletion

  • On-device data is kept until you delete it in the App (e.g. delete items) or clear app data / uninstall the App from your device.
  • Google may retain data according to its own retention rules for ads, purchases, and Play services.

Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the age required by your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we should remove information, contact us using the Play Store listing or developer contact.

International users

If you use the App outside your country of residence, data may be processed in countries where Google and its subprocessors operate, as described in their policies.

Your choices

  • Use Premium or device/ad settings to reduce or remove advertising where applicable.
  • Revoke notification permission in Android settings.
  • Uninstall the App to remove locally stored data from the device.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The Last updated date at the top will change; for material changes we will provide notice as required by law or Google Play policies.

Contact

Use the developer contact shown on the Google Play store page for Home stock, or the support channel listed there.

This document is provided for transparency. It is not legal advice.
